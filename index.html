About Our Club

Founded in 2024, the Campus Gaming Club is a community of students who share a passion for video games. We welcome gamers of all skill levels and interests!

Our club meets every Tuesday and Thursday in the Student Union Building, Room 205. We have gaming PCs, consoles, and a chill atmosphere where you can relax and play with friends.

Whether you're into competitive esports, casual gaming, or just want to make new friends, you'll find your place here!
