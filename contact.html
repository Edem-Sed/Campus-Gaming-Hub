Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need to be good at games to join?

Not at all! We welcome gamers of all skill levels. Whether you're a beginner or a pro, you'll find people to play with and have fun.

Is there a membership fee?

No! Membership is completely free for all students. We're funded by the student activities office.

Do I need to bring my own equipment?

We have gaming PCs, consoles, controllers, and headsets available for members to use. However, you're welcome to bring your own gear if you prefer.

Can I join if I don't have much free time?

Absolutely! There's no attendance requirement. Come whenever you can and enjoy the community at your own pace.
